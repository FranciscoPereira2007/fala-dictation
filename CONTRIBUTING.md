# Contributing

Start with `swift test` and the build commands in the README. Keep pull requests
focused on a reproducible problem. Include the device/OS, steps to reproduce,
expected/actual result and the checks you ran. Real-device reports should state
whether paste was enabled and whether the first model download had completed.

Do not attach private recordings, credentials, pairing codes or customer data.
Synthetic audio or a short recording you explicitly choose to share is preferable.
Do not claim a feature works on hardware based solely on a successful build.

The current focus is reliable local-network iPhone-to-Mac dictation. Discuss
larger features in an issue before implementing them. Contributions are submitted
under this repository's MIT license; preserve existing third-party notices.
